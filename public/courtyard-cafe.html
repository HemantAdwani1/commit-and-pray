Our Story

A courtyard worth
lingering in

The Courtyard Cafe began as a single table under a neem tree and a simple promise — good ingredients, unhurried mornings, and coffee worth waking up for. The courtyard has since grown to hold string lights, potted jasmine, and a kitchen that starts every day with fresh dough, but the promise hasn't changed.

Whether you're in for a quick flat white before work or a long Sunday brunch with friends, pull up a chair. This is a place built for staying a while.

  • Seasonal, locally-sourced ingredients
  • Slow-brewed coffee, roasted in small batches
  • A courtyard made for lingering, not rushing
